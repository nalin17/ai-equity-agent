# ADR-009 - India macro from MoSPI's API, and declared market calendars

- decision_id: ADR-009
- date: 2026-10-04
- problem: Stage 10C-2 (architecture 40B step 9a, 3G, 5C rule 4) needs India's own macro statistics -
  FRED's India series are stale or two months late - and a way to prove that a foreign market was
  closed. FRED's own listing cannot prove it: it shows the Nasdaq Composite on Good Friday
  19-Apr-2019 and the Nikkei 225 on 1-Oct-2020 (the Tokyo exchange's all-day outage), each the
  previous close repeated.
- research (2026-10-03/04; samples fetched with the owner's approval, 12 requests):
  - MoSPI's eSankhyiki terms: MoSPI's own datasets are Category A under the Government Statistics
    Data Dissemination policy 2026 - reuse, commercial or not, with prominent attribution. Data
    from other ministries follows their terms: WPI is compiled by the Office of the Economic
    Adviser (DPIIT), whose site publishes no reuse terms; RBI tables follow RBI's terms (ADR-006).
  - MoSPI's API (https://api.mospi.gov.in) answers without a key or token. The ministry's own
    open-source client (github.com/nso-india/esankhyiki-mcp, MIT) calls it the same way, but it
    also switches certificate checking off; that is not copied. The server needs TLS 'legacy
    renegotiation'; its certificate verifies normally.
  - Real answers: CPI base 2024 (2025 onwards), its back series 2013-2024 recalculated on base
    2024, IIP base 2022-23 (2023 onwards), GDP base 2022-23 quarterly (April-June 2022 onwards).
    No publication time; no provisional or final marks (the GDP 'revision' field is empty). The
    national-accounts indicator list answers HTTP 500; the ministry's client ships the indicator
    codes (GDP = 5) for exactly that reason.
  - exchange_calendars 4.13.2 (Apache License 2.0, 670+ stars) holds New York, Tokyo and NSE/BSE
    calendars compiled from the exchanges' notices. Checked: NSE's official 2026 trading holidays
    16 of 16; FRED's Nasdaq 2016-2026 102 of 103 closed days and Nikkei 180 of 181 (the
    exceptions are the two repeated closes above). VIX (Cboe computes it on some US holidays) and
    the bond-market series follow other calendars.
- options: (1) hand downloads from MoSPI; (2) MoSPI's API; (3) wait for the NSE research data;
  for calendars: (a) trust FRED's listing; (b) hand-maintained lists; (c) lists generated from the
  open-source library, reviewed and committed.
- decision: (2) and (c), approved by the owner on 2026-10-04, as a narrow addition to ADR-005,
  ADR-006 and ADR-008:
  - src/ingestion/news_fetch.py may also ask api.mospi.gov.in for exactly /api/cpi/getCPIData,
    /api/iip/getIipData and /api/nas/getNASData - https only, no redirects, at least 3 seconds
    apart, stopping on HTTP 429;
  - TLS legacy renegotiation is switched on for that one host only; certificates and host names
    are checked for every host, and no code may switch checking off (static test);
  - series are declared in config/india_macro_series.yaml (recorded once, never silently
    changed); every answer page is kept byte for byte; a row not matching the declared filters
    refuses the whole answer;
  - MoSPI values are current-decision evidence from this system's first read; historical replay
    stays in AVAILABILITY_REVIEW; a back series is 'reconstructed' and never stands in for what
    was known at the time; each base year is its own series;
  - attribution "Source: MoSPI, National Statistics Office - eSankhyiki" is printed by
    fetch-india and show-india;
  - config/market_calendars.yaml is generated by data/handover/tools/make_calendar_yaml.py from
    exchange_calendars and reviewed; the Apache licence text is kept in
    docs/third_party/exchange_calendars-LICENSE.txt; the library itself is not a dependency of
    this project and never runs in it;
  - for a series mapped to a calendar, a value on a closed day is never used and no value on a
    trading day is a source conflict; the stored vintage stays as FRED gave it.
- reason: official, free, documented data with the owner's licensed-only rule (2026-10-03);
  calendars that are checked against the exchange and against real data, instead of a source's
  own listing that is wrong on real days.
- impact: fetch-india asks about 7 pages a day (seconds). The calendars end on 2026-12-31:
  show-macro warns 60 days before; regenerate them with the next year's exchange notices.
  Not used: WPI until its publisher's terms are found; RBI; India VIX and Nifty history (awaiting
  NSE's answer to the research-data request).
- supersedes: none (adds to ADR-005, ADR-006 and ADR-008)
- owner: project owner
